What you can do

Combine media features

Merge videos

Add several clips and they play back to back in the order you choose.

Merge images

Put two or more images side by side into one picture.

Slideshow video

Turn your images into a video, with 1 to 6 seconds per slide.

Reorder

Move any item up or down, or remove it, before you merge.

Private

Your files stay in your browser tab.

Works offline

Use it with no internet after your first visit.

How to

Use the combine media in 4 steps

  1. 1

    Add your files

    Choose several videos or images from your computer.

  2. 2

    Put them in order

    Use the arrows to move items. Remove any you do not need.

  3. 3

    Merge

    Press Merge videos, Merge into one image, or Create slideshow video.

  4. 4

    Download

    Save the result to your computer.

Good to know

  • Merging videos plays them in real time, so a 10 minute result takes about 10 minutes.
  • For best results use clips with the same shape (for example all 16:9).

FAQ

Questions about the combine media

How do I join two videos online for free?

Add both videos in the Combine tool, put them in order and press Merge videos.

Can I make a slideshow from photos?

Yes. Add your images, choose how long each slide shows and press Create slideshow video.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.

Can I mix different video sizes?

You can, but results look best when the clips have the same shape and size.

What file do I get?

Videos and slideshows are saved as WebM. Merged images are saved as a picture.
