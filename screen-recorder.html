What you can do

Screen recorder features

Screen, window or tab

Choose exactly what to share. Perfect for tutorials, bug reports, demos and recorded calls.

System sound and microphone

Record the sound from your computer, your own voice, or both at the same time.

Webcam bubble

Add a camera bubble so viewers can see you. Choose the corner, the size, a circle or rounded square, and whether to mirror it.

Gradient background and rounded corners

Place your screen on a soft gradient with space around it and rounded corners, so a plain recording looks ready to share.

Save straight to a file

In Chrome and Edge, the recording is written to a file on your disk as it happens, so long recordings do not fill up memory.

Pause, countdown and timer

Pause and resume without cutting, get a 3 or 5 second countdown, and watch the live timer.

Quality and frame rate

Ask for 1080p or 720p, and 15, 30 or 60 frames per second. Show or hide the mouse cursor.

Live captions

Turn on speech to text and your words become captions you can edit and burn into the video.

How to

Use the screen recorder in 4 steps

  1. 1

    Choose what to record

    Open the recorder, tick the options you want (webcam, system audio, microphone, captions) and press Start recording.

  2. 2

    Pick a screen

    Your browser shows its own picker. Choose a whole screen, one window or one tab. Turn on Share audio if you want the sound.

  3. 3

    Record

    After the countdown, talk and show what you need. Use Pause to take a break. Press Stop when you are done.

  4. 4

    Save or edit

    Download the video, or open it in the editor to trim, blur, crop or turn it into a GIF.

Good to know

  • Screen recording needs a desktop browser such as Chrome or Edge. Phones and tablets do not allow it.
  • On a Mac, browsers usually share the sound of one tab only, not the whole system.
  • Your recording lives in the tab until you download it, so save it before you close the page.

FAQ

Questions about the screen recorder

Is this screen recorder really free?

Yes. There is no sign up, no time limit we add, and no watermark. The only limit is your own computer.

Does it upload my recording?

No. The video is created and kept in your browser tab. It is never sent to a server.

Can I record my screen with sound?

Yes. Tick System or Tab Audio for computer sound and Microphone for your voice, then choose Share audio in the browser prompt.

Can I record a Zoom, Meet or Teams call?

Yes, if you share the call window or tab and turn on audio sharing. Tell everyone on the call that you are recording.

Which browser works best?

Chrome and Edge on Windows, Mac, Linux or Chromebook give the most options, including system audio on Windows.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after your first visit the app is saved on your device and works with no internet.

Can I add a webcam bubble and a nice background?

Yes. Tick Webcam bubble, choose its corner, size and shape, and pick a gradient background with rounded corners. A live preview shows how the video will look.

Can I record for a long time without running out of memory?

In Chrome and Edge, tick Save to a file while recording. The video is written to your disk as you record. In other browsers it stays in memory until you download it, so keep long recordings short.
